# Skylark Exim Cinematic Scroll Storyboard

This file defines the scroll animation storyline.

## Master Scroll Shape

The full page is one continuous cinematic voyage:

`Port -> Boarding -> Compass -> Open Bay -> Nets -> Catch -> Chilled Hold -> Return -> Transfer -> Processing -> QC -> Freezing -> Packing -> Cold Storage -> Reefer -> Container Vessel -> Globe -> RFQ`

## Scene 1 - Opening Port

### Visual

Bright 10 AM light over Visakhapatnam port. A large fishing vessel is docked. A bigger container vessel can be seen deeper in the port. Water glints. Cranes move slowly. Workers prepare equipment.

### Camera

Start low near wet dock surface, then dolly forward toward the vessel.

### Copy

`SKYLARK EXIM`

`A shipment begins long before the container is sealed.`

### Motion

Water moves. Ropes sway. Crew silhouettes board the vessel.

## Scene 2 - Fishermen Board

### Visual

The fishing crew boards the vessel with gear, insulated crates, nets, and ice boxes. The ship feels large and capable.

### Camera

Move from dock to deck in one smooth scroll-controlled push.

### Copy

`The Bay is not a supplier. It is a test.`

### Motion

Crew movement, deck preparation, nets loaded, engine vibration.

## Scene 3 - Compass And Conditions

### Visual

A premium compass/navigation overlay appears over the ocean and vessel.

### Data Elements

- Time: `10:00 AM`
- Sea temperature
- Storage temperature
- Wind direction
- Route heading
- Bay of Bengal coordinates
- Distance from Vizag
- Catch status: `Outbound`

### Camera

Rise above the vessel, still tracking forward.

### Copy

`Time. Temperature. Direction. Discipline.`

## Scene 4 - Into The Bay Of Bengal

### Visual

The ship moves into blue-green open water. Waves build enough to feel adventurous but not unsafe. Clouds move overhead with shafts of sunlight.

### Camera

Alternate scroll movement between sky view and sea-level tracking.

### Copy

`Into the Bay of Bengal.`

`Where the ocean gives only once.`

## Scene 5 - Nets Into The Water

### Visual

Fishermen release nets from the vessel. Nets expand and move through water. Use above-water and underwater glimpses.

### Camera

Follow the net line as it arcs from deck into the sea.

### Copy

`The catch begins with timing.`

### Motion

Net rope tension, water splash, underwater particles, sunlight through water.

## Scene 6 - Swordfish And Tuna Catch

### Visual

Swordfish and tuna are the hero seafood. Show them with cinematic respect, cleanly and commercially, not in a graphic or messy way.

### Focus

Primary:

- Swordfish.
- Tuna.

Secondary:

- Other ocean fish.
- Shrimp appears later in the pond/product chapter.

### Camera

Close-ups of fish form and scale. Wide deck moment showing catch being handled.

### Copy

`Swordfish and tuna are the heroes of the ocean route.`

`Handled for export from the first moment on deck.`

## Scene 7 - Onboard Chilled Storage

### Visual

Catch moves into onboard chilled storage. Show clean insulated storage, ice, temperature readouts, and organized handling.

### Camera

Move from deck to storage hatch/hold.

### Copy

`The cold chain starts at sea.`

`Freshness is protected before the harbour appears.`

### Data Overlay

- Storage temperature.
- Catch lot.
- Time since catch.
- Vessel route.

## Scene 8 - Return To Port

### Visual

The vessel returns to Vizag. The camera approaches port from ocean side. Cranes, docks, and containers grow larger.

### Camera

Sky view to deck view to dock-level view.

### Copy

`The catch returns to the coast.`

`The standard continues on land.`

## Scene 9 - Transfer To Cold-Chain Containers

### Visual

Fish move from vessel storage to cold-chain transport containers. Show forklifts, insulated bins, clean workers, and temperature discipline.

### Camera

Follow one container/bin as the hero object.

### Copy

`From vessel hold to cold-chain transfer.`

### Motion

Container moves along a path. Temperature overlay remains visible.

## Scene 10 - Processing Facility Arrival

### Visual

The cold-chain container enters the processing facility. Bright, clean industrial space. Stainless steel. Inspection lamps.

### Camera

Container-door wipe into facility.

### Copy

`Every product earns the next room.`

## Scene 11 - Fish Cutting And Portioning

### Visual

Swordfish and tuna are cleaned, cut, portioned, and prepared. Keep the visuals premium and not graphic.

### Camera

Controlled close-ups of knives, boards, stainless tables, portions, and gloved hands.

### Copy

`Skill turns ocean scale into buyer specification.`

## Scene 12 - Pond And Shrimp Origin Chapter

### Visual

The ocean journey briefly opens into a second-origin chapter: Andhra aquaculture ponds. Water sampling, pond grid, batch tag, harvest crate.

### Purpose

This chapter preserves the Skylark idea of two origins.

### Copy

`The pond is a controlled history.`

`Water. Inputs. Harvest. Batch identity.`

## Scene 13 - Shrimp Product Forms

### Visual

Show shrimp forms as elegant product transitions:

- HOSO.
- HLSO.
- PUD.
- PTO.

### Copy

`A buyer who names a form is naming a promise of consistency.`

## Scene 14 - Grading And Sorting

### Visual

Conveyors and sorting. Product sizes separate. Grade overlays appear.

### Grades

- 10/20.
- 21/25.
- 26/30.
- 31/40.
- 41/50.

### Copy

`The number on the label is an agreement.`

## Scene 15 - QC And Residue Testing

### Visual

Slow down. The QC inspector becomes the central character. Show lab instruments, vials, inspection lights, HACCP/EIC documents, and residue certificates.

### Copy

`Trust is not a claim. It is a result.`

`Nitrofurans. Chloramphenicol. Tetracyclines.`

`Every batch is tested before shipment.`

`EU and US limits, whichever is stricter.`

`The certificate travels with the carton.`

## Scene 16 - Freezing And Glazing

### Visual

IQF tunnel, block freezing, blast freezing, frost, cold vapour, clean glaze.

### Camera

Macro product close-up. Frost forms as scroll advances.

### Copy

`Where time stops.`

`A clean glaze protects the journey.`

## Scene 17 - Packing And Cold Storage

### Visual

Cartons filled, weighed, labelled, sealed, stacked, and moved into cold storage.

### Copy

`A carton is not packaging. It is proof.`

`Minus 18 to minus 20 degrees Celsius.`

`700 pallets of cold-chain readiness.`

## Scene 18 - Export Documents

### Visual

Export officer prepares documents. Documents animate into a clean stack, then into route lines.

### Documents

- Health certificate.
- Packing list.
- Certificate of origin.
- Residue certificate.
- Compliance records.
- HACCP/EIC documents.

### Copy

`A shipment moves on paperwork that is correct.`

## Scene 19 - Reefer Container Loading

### Visual

Packed cartons move into reefer containers. Temperature is set. Doors close. Seal bar drops.

### Copy

`The promise is now under seal.`

## Scene 20 - Container Vessel Departure

### Visual

Containers move through port and load onto a huge container vessel. Use container vessel, not cruise ship, for accuracy. It can still feel massive and cinematic.

### Camera

Top-down sky view. Containers align like a precise grid.

### Copy

`From Visakhapatnam to the world's table.`

## Scene 21 - Globe And Global Routes

### Visual

The top-down port view transforms into a globe. India glows. Route lines arc outward to world markets.

### Camera

Pull back from ship to map to globe.

### Copy

`From this shore to yours.`

`Two origins. One standard.`

## Scene 22 - RFQ

### Visual

The final scene remains cinematic: globe, ocean, route lines, and sealed container remain in background while the RFQ appears as a buyer console.
